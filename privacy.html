2. How we use your data

3. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers who help us operate — for example our payment processor (Safepay), hosting provider, and communication tools (WhatsApp Business, email) — and only to the extent needed to deliver our Services.

4. Cookies & analytics

We use basic, privacy-friendly analytics to understand how visitors use the Site (page views and general traffic patterns). This does not identify you personally.

5. Data retention

We keep project and payment records for as long as needed to fulfil our legal, accounting and support obligations, typically for 3 years, after which they are securely deleted.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time by emailing info@websoulstudio.net.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including using a PCI-compliant payment processor for all card transactions.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
